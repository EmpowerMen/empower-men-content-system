#### Text

We believe that the many ways in which the Magician has appeared in history and in which he appears among men today are mere fragments of a once-whole image. That primordial Magician in men has manifested itself most fully in what anthropologists refer to as the shaman. The shaman in traditional societies was the healer, the one who
restored life. who found lost souls, and who discovered the hidden causes of misfortune. He was the one who restored wholeness and fullness of being to both individuals and communities. Indeed. the Magician energy today still has the same ultimate aim. The Magician. and the shaman as his fullest human vessel, aims at fullness of being for all things, through the compassionate application of knowledge and
technology.
