#### Text

It’s easy to understand why so many of us have an over-functioning warrior archetype. 
When our work environments are infused with military metaphors like strategy, tactics, competition, objective – this frames the daily grind in terms of war.
This will activate the warrior part of your psyche, and an over-stimulation of this function for too long can create an imbalance.
The price we pay for deployment in such endless battles is the inevitable burnout. You end up being both The Sadist and The Masochist, driving yourself into the ground working punishing hours and neglecting the finer things in life.
To counter this, self-care routines for relaxation, nutrition, exercise and meditation have to come in to play. Your Lover archetype can help you with this and temper the out of control Warrior within you.
