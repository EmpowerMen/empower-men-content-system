#### Text

Our obligation - and I include in "our" all the women and men writing about gender - is to describe masculine in such a way that it does not exclude the masculine in women, and yet hits a resonant string in the man's heart. No one says there aren't resonating strings in a woman's heart too - but in the man's heart there is a low string that makes his whole chest tremble when the qualities of the masculine are spoken of in the right way.
