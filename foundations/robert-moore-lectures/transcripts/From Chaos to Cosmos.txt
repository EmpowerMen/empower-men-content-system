## Lecture Context

In this lecture, Dr. Moore covers:  
- Mythology as an expression of human nature  
- The sacred centre (axis mundi) and its psychological significance  
- The dynamics of grandiosity, creative power, and fragmentation  
- The trickster archetype’s balancing role  
- Rituals as transformers of archetypal energy  
- The eternal return and its meaning for personal growth  
- Integration of masculine energies through myth

**Audience:** Academic Psychologists  
**Format:** Seminar  
**Related reading:** *King, Warrior, Magician, Lover*
