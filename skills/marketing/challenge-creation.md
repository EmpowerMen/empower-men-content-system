# Challenge Creation Guidelines

## Overview

Challenges are structured, time-bound practices that help men apply archetypal concepts to real-world action. They transform theory into measurable behavior change.

## Challenge Structure

### Required Elements

#### 1\. Challenge Title

Clear, action-oriented title that indicates the archetype and focus area.

**Format:** \[Archetype\]'s \[Focus Area\] Challenge

**Examples:**

- The Warrior’s Boundary Challenge
- The King’s Decision Challenge
- The Magician’s Reflection Challenge

#### 2\. Duration

Specify exact timeframe (typically 7, 14, or 30 days).

#### 3\. Archetype Focus

State which archetype from Robert Moore’s framework (King, Warrior, Magician, Lover) and briefly reference the framework.

#### 4\. Challenge Overview

2-3 paragraphs that:

- Identify the problem or gap
- Connect to the archetype
- State what the participant will do

#### 5\. The Practice

Break down the challenge into phases with specific daily or phase-based actions.

**Structure:**

- **Phase 1:** Action + what to look for
- **Phase 2:** Action + what to look for
- **Phase 3:** Action + what to look for

Each phase should have clear, actionable instructions.

#### 6\. Shadow Work Warning

Identify both shadow poles (inflated and deflated) for the archetype.

**Format:**

- **\[Shadow Name\]** (Archetype+): Description of inflated shadow behavior
- **\[Shadow Name\]** (Archetype-): Description of deflated shadow behavior

Include guidance on the mature archetype response.

#### 7\. Success Metric

Clear, measurable criteria for completion. Participants should know definitively whether they completed the challenge.

#### 8\. Reflection Questions

3-5 questions that help participants integrate the experience and notice patterns.

## Writing Guidelines for Challenges

### Voice

- Direct and actionable
- No coddling or softening
- Assume the reader is capable
- Use “you” to make it personal
- Command form for instructions

### Structure

- One idea per sentence
- Maximum 25 words per sentence
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Use bold for emphasis on key actions
- Avoid numbered lists in body text where possible (use phases or day ranges instead)

### Language

- Active voice
- Present tense for instructions
- Concrete, specific actions
- Avoid abstract concepts without grounding them in behavior

### What to Avoid

- Em-dash rhetorical pivots
- “X isn’t about X, it’s about Y” formulas
- Over-explaining or justifying
- Apologetic language
- Vague instructions

## Example: The Warrior’s Boundary Challenge

### Core Challenge Document

**Challenge Title:** The Warrior’s Boundary Challenge

**Duration:** 7 Days

**Archetype Focus:** Warrior Energy (Robert Moore’s King, Warrior, Magician, Lover framework)

**Challenge Overview:**  
Most men confuse being “nice” with being strong. They tolerate disrespect, energy drain, and misalignment, then wonder why they feel powerless. The mature Warrior knows how to define and defend sacred space through clear boundaries.

This week, you’ll identify, state, and hold one boundary that you’ve been avoiding.

**The Practice:**

**Day 1-2: Identify**  
Where are you tolerating what you shouldn’t? Look for:

- Disrespect from others
- Energy drain (people, commitments, habits)
- Misalignment with your values
- Situations where you say “yes” but mean “no”

Choose ONE boundary to work with this week.

**Day 3-4: State It**  
Communicate your boundary clearly. No apologies. No over-explaining.

“I’m not available for that” is a complete sentence.

Practice stating it out loud before you need to use it.

**Day 5-7: Hold It**  
The Warrior doesn’t negotiate his boundaries. He enforces them calmly and consistently.

When tested (and you will be tested), hold the line without drama.

**Shadow Work Warning:**  
Watch for these shadow patterns:

**The Sadist** (Warrior+): Cruel, punishing, using boundaries as weapons to inflict pain

**The Masochist** (Warrior-): Collapsing, no boundaries, tolerating abuse

The mature Warrior holds the line without cruelty or collapse.

**Success Metric:**  
You’ve completed this challenge when you:

1. Identified one specific boundary
2. Stated it clearly to the relevant person/situation
3. Held it for the full week without negotiating

**Reflection Questions:**

- What did you notice when you stated your boundary?
- How did others respond?
- What shadow patterns showed up for you?
- How does it feel to respect yourself through boundaries?

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing a challenge, verify:

- \[ \] Clear archetype connection with accurate shadow poles
- \[ \] Specific, measurable success criteria
- \[ \] Actionable daily/phase instructions
- \[ \] All three channel versions created
- \[ \] No prohibited writing patterns used
- \[ \] Reflection questions included
- \[ \] Duration clearly stated
- \[ \] Engagement prompts in each version
- \[ \] Follows ECS voice and style guidelines
- \[ \] Shadow work accurately represents Moore’s framework
