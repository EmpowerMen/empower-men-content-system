# RUBRIC.md

## Pass/Fail Criteria for YouTube Content

### Thumbnail Headline  
- Length: 5-7 words  
- Clear, compelling, and relevant to video topic  
- Avoid clickbait or misleading language  
- Uses archetype or KWML references where appropriate

### Video Title  
- Length: 50-60 characters  
- Includes primary SEO keywords front-loaded  
- Clearly conveys video benefit or hook  
- No clickbait or exaggeration

### Video Description  
- First 150 characters hook the viewer  
- Summarizes video content accurately  
- Includes timestamps if applicable  
- Contains relevant links and CTAs  
- Uses natural keyword integration  
- Includes relevant hashtags  
- Complies with YouTube policies and EmpowerMen voice/tone
